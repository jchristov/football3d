# Credits

## Crowd sounds
`public/audio/*.mp3` are trimmed excerpts of **"Free Crowd Cheering Sounds"** by **Gregor Quendel**
(<https://opengameart.org/content/free-crowd-cheering-sounds>, <https://www.gregorquendel.com>),
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: trimmed, faded and re-encoded to MP3.

Mapping: `bed.mp3` = "10 - Ambience"; `goal1-3.mp3` = "04 Strong cheering II", "03 Strong cheering I",
"02 Strong cheering and soft rhythmic cheering"; `chance*.mp3` = "06 / 05 Soft cheering".

## Goal post / crossbar impact sounds
`public/audio/post_*.mp3` are trimmed and normalised excerpts of **"100 CC0 Metal and Wood SFX"** from OpenGameArt
(<https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx>), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No attribution is required for CC0 works. Used files: `metal_hit_03`, `metal_hit_05`, `metal_slam_01`.
